Part 1: Camera Positioning & Lighting

Our vision system tracks 33 body coordinates in real time. Accurate angle measurement requires a clean camera shot:

  • Distance: Stand 6 to 8 feet away so your limbs fit inside the frame.
  • Height: Position your device at waist or chest height (avoid filming from the floor angled up).
  • Lighting: Keep the room light in front of you so your body doesn't become a dark silhouette.

Part 2: Two-Stage Rep Counting

To prevent fake or jittery rep increments, the AI uses a locked state machine:

DOWN STAGE → Rep is primed only when you reach full contraction/depth.
UP STAGE → Rep counts (+1) once you return to full lockout extension.

⚡ Half-reps and bouncing will not trigger a count. Strict form is required!

Part 3: Exercise Movement Rules

💪 Bicep Curls (Dual Arm)

Stand face-forward. Fully extend arms past > 160° at the bottom, then curl upwards past < 35°. Both arms are tracked independently.

🦵 Squats (Single Leg Reference)

Stand sideways to the camera. Lower until your knee breaks parallel at < 90°, then stand tall until past > 160°.
