About AI Gym Trainer

AI Gym Trainer utilizes lightweight MediaPipe Pose machine learning architectures running via OpenCV and Python WebSockets.

Joint angles are calculated dynamically via 2D spatial trigonometry ($arctan2$) to track state transitions and detect full ranges of motion without latency.
